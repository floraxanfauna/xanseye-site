# Owner guide (the 5-minute version)
Sign in at `/admin` with xanflorafauna@gmail.com.
- **Today**: next session, today's schedule, "Needs attention" list, printable run sheet.
- **Availability → Weekly schedule** (like Calendly): switch on the weekdays you shoot, set hours (add morning + afternoon ranges, copy a day to others), pick session length and break, add date overrides for holidays or extra days, check the live preview, then **Publish**. Tick "keep adding new dates automatically" and it fills in future weeks for you. Changed your hours? Tick "Replace unbooked times" before publishing. Booked sessions are never touched, and times you closed stay closed. **Pick individual dates** is for one-off days.
- **Page editor**: change wording, colors, photos, prices, FAQs → Save draft → check Preview → Publish. Duplicate a season for the next holiday. Old versions can be restored.
- **Sessions**: every booking, their answers, your private notes, gallery link, reschedule/cancel.
- **Pause bookings**: top-right button.
Anything "waiting for setup" runs by itself once Google/email is connected. Client answers are always saved first.
